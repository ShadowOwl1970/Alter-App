Character management made simple

Stay focused on the story.

Alter brings your character library and proxying tools together in one place so switching identities during roleplay feels effortless.

01

Manage Characters

Create, edit, organize, search, and delete characters from a dedicated Character Manager.

02

Custom Proxies

Give characters prefixes, suffixes, and aliases so you can quickly switch between them while roleplaying.

03

Autoproxy

Select an active character and let Alter automatically proxy normal messages while you stay immersed in the scene.

04

Character Avatars

Give every character an avatar so your library remains easy to recognize and visually organized.

05

Character Groups

Organize characters into groups for campaigns, casts, fandoms, stories, or whatever fits your roleplay.

06

Root Account Linking

Link your Character Manager with your Root account so Alter knows which character library belongs to you.

Built specifically for Root

Alter is designed around Root communities and roleplay workflows rather than adapting a tool made for another platform.
